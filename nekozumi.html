Nekozumi

Nekozumi is a simple, free and open-source manga reader for Android 5.0+ from the Nekomo project.

What it is

  • A lightweight, no-frills manga reader — free and open source.
  • Runs on Android 5.0 and up, so it works on just about everything.
  • Built as a companion to Nekomo's manga features.
