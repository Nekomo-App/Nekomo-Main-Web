NEX

NEX (net.nekomo.nex) is the free companion app for N𝖊kō𝖒𝖔 — it hands source repositories straight into N𝖊kō𝖒𝖔. Tap a repository link in NEX and N𝖊kō𝖒𝖔 opens the nekomo-repo://add flow to confirm and add it, instead of you copying URLs by hand.

What it does

  • Browse and send repository links to N𝖊kō𝖒𝖔 in one tap — N𝖊kō𝖒𝖔 still asks you to confirm every add, so nothing installs silently.
  • N𝖊kō𝖒𝖔 detects NEX automatically and can open it from Settings → Extensions.
  • Free and open source, same as N𝖊kō𝖒𝖔 itself.

How they talk

NEX opens nekomo-repo://add?url=<repository> links; N𝖊kō𝖒𝖔 registers that scheme and shows its normal add-repository confirmation. No account, no pairing, nothing sent to our servers.
