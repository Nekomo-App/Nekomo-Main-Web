Companion app

NEX

NEX (net.nekomo.nex) is the free companion app for Nekomo. It hands source repositories straight into Nekomo — tap a repository link in NEX and Nekomo opens the nekomo-repo://add flow to confirm and add it, instead of you copying URLs by hand.

What it does

  • Browse and send repository links to Nekomo in one tap (Nekomo still asks you to confirm every add — nothing installs silently).
  • Nekomo detects NEX automatically and can open it from Settings → Extensions.
  • Free and open source, same as Nekomo itself.

Get NEX

NEX is a separate app and is not bundled inside Nekomo. Grab it from the NEX repository:

Download NEX (GitHub releases) Source code

If the releases page is empty, NEX builds are being prepared — check back later or build it from source. NEX is optional: every repository can also be added by URL inside Nekomo (Settings → Extensions → add repository).

How they talk

NEX opens nekomo-repo://add?url=<repository> links; Nekomo registers that scheme and shows its normal add-repository confirmation. No account, no pairing, nothing sent to our servers.

Nekomo — community-run, open source. NEX and Nekomo are separate apps by the same project.
