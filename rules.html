Community Rules

Keep Nekomo a friendly place. These rules apply in the app, on Discord and Telegram, and on GitHub.

Last updated: October 15, 2026

The short version: Be respectful to other users, avoid harassment or inappropriate content, and do not spam. By using Nekomo and joining our community you agree to follow these rules.

Contents

  1. Be respectful
  2. No harassment or hate
  3. No NSFW / illegal content
  4. No spam or self-promo
  5. No piracy distribution
  6. Support etiquette
  7. Beta testing conduct

  9. Reporting

1. Be respectful

  • Treat every member, helper, and developer with courtesy, even when you disagree.
  • Keep conversation civil and in English where staff can moderate it (or use the language channel provided).
  • No personal attacks, flame wars, or “drama” brought in from other communities.

2. No harassment or hate

  • No harassment, stalking, doxxing, threats, or encouraging self-harm.
  • No hate speech or discrimination based on race, ethnicity, gender, sexuality, religion, disability, or nationality.
  • Do not share anyone’s private information without consent.

3. No NSFW or illegal content

  • No sexual content involving minors, ever. This is an immediate permanent ban and will be reported.
  • Keep explicit, gory, or shocking material out of community spaces.
  • No malware, phishing links, scams, or anything illegal in your jurisdiction.

4. No spam or self-promo

  • No repeated messages, mass pings, chain posts, or unsolicited DMs.
  • No advertising of other servers, apps, services, referral links, or crypto.
  • Do not impersonate staff, developers, or the Nekomo brand.

5. No piracy distribution

  • Nekomo does not host or provide content. Do not post direct links to pirated media, paid-content leaks, or “where to watch free” lists in our spaces.
  • Do not ask staff for specific streaming sources or extension repositories. Support for third-party providers is not offered.
  • Do not post cracked, modded, or re-signed copies of Nekomo. Share only official builds from the Beta page or the GitHub repositories.

Read the DMCA & Disclaimer for how Nekomo handles copyright concerns.

6. Support etiquette

  • Search the Support page and previous messages before asking.
  • Include your app version, Android version, device, and steps to reproduce. Attach the in-app Error log when you can (review it for private data first).
  • Be patient. Nekomo is volunteer-run with no guaranteed response time, so please don’t ping developers repeatedly.

7. Beta testing conduct

  • Beta/debug builds are unfinished. Report bugs constructively, one issue per report.
  • Do not leak beta builds outside official channels, and do not post private keys, tokens, or personal logs in public issues.
  • Security vulnerabilities should be reported privately to the maintainers, not posted publicly.

8. Staff & enforcement

Moderators may remove content and warn, mute, kick, or ban members at their discretion, with or without notice. Staff decisions are final, though you can appeal politely in a private message. Ignorance of the rules is not an excuse, and attempting to evade a ban leads to a permanent ban. These rules may change at any time.

9. Reporting

See someone breaking the rules? Report it to a moderator on Discord or Telegram and include a link or screenshot. For app problems, use Support. For copyright notices, follow the process on the DMCA page.
