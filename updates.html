Obtainium

Nekomo's releases are marked as prereleases on GitHub, so the in-app update checker (and most APK stores) won't pick them up automatically. Obtainium can track them for you:

  1. Open Obtainium → Add App → paste https://github.com/Nekomo-App/Nekomo
  2. Enable Include prereleases in additional settings
  3. Save — you'll get a notification on every new build
