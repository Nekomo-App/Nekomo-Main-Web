Usage rules

✅ Please do

  • Use the logos unmodified, at their original proportions.
  • Keep clear space around the mark so it doesn't feel cramped next to other text or logos.
  • Use them to refer to the real Nekomo project — app listings, articles, videos, community server icons, etc.
  • Link back to nekomoapp.netlify.app or the GitHub org when used externally, where practical.

🚫 Please don't

  • Recolor, distort, stretch, rotate, or add effects to the marks.
  • Use the logo in a way that implies official endorsement, sponsorship, or partnership that doesn't exist.
  • Combine the Nekomo mark with another app/brand's logo in a way that implies affiliation.
  • Use the logo to promote a modified, cracked, or re-signed build of the app (see the Community Rules).
  • Use the logo in a way that violates the Terms of Service or DMCA & Disclaimer.
Note on trademark status: Nekomo is a community-run, open-source project and these assets are original artwork made for it — they are not a registered trademark. That means these usage rules are a courtesy request, not a legal trademark license, though misuse that causes confusion about affiliation may still raise other legal issues (see Trademarks & Brand Concerns). Questions about using these assets? Ask in Discord or via Support.
