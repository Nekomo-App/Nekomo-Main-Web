NekoShield

NekoShield is an all-in-one Android network privacy and management app from the Nekomo project: DNS, VPN, proxy, ad & tracker blocking, traffic filtering and network tools — for phones and Android TV.

What it does

  • Custom DNS, VPN and proxy routing with per-app control.
  • Ad & tracker blocking and traffic filtering at the network layer.
  • Network tools and diagnostics — for Android mobile and Android TV.
